WHY THE NAME SUNDAI

Healthy AI, from name to practice.

In Danish, sund means healthy. SundAI stands for AI that is secure, responsible, useful and human-centred — not technology for its own sake.

About SundAI

Independent, cross-disciplinary AI initiative

AI governance · security · human-centred technology

SundAI was created to bring together AI governance, cybersecurity, organisational risk and human-centred implementation in a practical European framework.
