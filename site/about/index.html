THE PERSON BEHIND SUNDAI

Technology starts with understanding the work.

I’m Eric Ariel Rimón. My background combines running businesses and leading teams with hands-on work in Python, API integrations, AI and cybersecurity. I built an operations assistant for my own restaurant business, so I know that a useful system has to work for the people using it.

SELECTED WORK

See what the work looks like.

Two projects with different kinds of evidence: an operations system in the founder’s own business and public website engineering you can inspect.

01 / FOUNDER’S OWN BUSINESS

Ariel’s Pizza · an operations assistant

The need
Bring ordering information, daily staff tasks and practical guidance into the same working process.
The work
Python and OpenAI connected ordering APIs with Trello, Google Sheets and WhatsApp. The assistant supported staff tasks and guidance, with human escalation for critical decisions.
The deliverable
An operational assistant transferred with the business sale in May 2025.
Evidence and limits

Based on the founder’s project account. This was a project in his own business. No independently measured time-saving or revenue claim is made.

02 / SUNDAI’S OWN PLATFORM

SundAI · enquiries and guide delivery

The need
Handle enquiries and requested guides with clear validation, failure handling and consent choices.
The work
Cloudflare Worker APIs validate requests, check Turnstile and route email through Resend. Guide follow-ups require confirmation and provide an unsubscribe path.
The deliverable
Public source code and automated tests covering validation, routing, provider failures and consent workflows.
Evidence and limits

The tests use synthetic data and mocked providers. They do not establish inbox delivery, uptime or conversion gains.
