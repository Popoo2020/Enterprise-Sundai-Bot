ABOUT SUNDAI

AI decisions need technical depth and human understanding.

SundAI is an independent European initiative focused on practical AI governance, security, responsible adoption and human-centred implementation. It is operated by RIIMON HOLDINGS LTD.

WHY THE NAME SUNDAI

Healthy AI, from name to practice.

In Danish, sund means healthy. SundAI stands for AI that is secure, responsible, useful and human-centred — not technology for its own sake.
