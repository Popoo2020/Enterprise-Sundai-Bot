FAQ

Direct answers for buyers and project owners.

Is this legal advice?

No. SundAI provides governance, security and implementation support. Legal interpretation should be confirmed with qualified counsel where required.

Does SundAI guarantee savings?

No. A baseline and measurement plan are used to evaluate time, cycle time, quality, consistency and adoption without promising a result before evidence exists.

Are ISO certifications claimed?

No organisational certification is claimed unless a current certificate explicitly covers the organisation and scope. Work can be standards-informed without overstating certification.
