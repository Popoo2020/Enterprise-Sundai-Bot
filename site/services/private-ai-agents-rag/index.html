Scope, responsibilities & boundaries

The fixed package

Private AI Assistant / RAG Pilot: one assistant, one ready-to-use knowledge source, one interface, one cloud provider, up to two user roles and up to ten pilot users. Agent actions are included only when explicitly described in the written scope.

Outside this scope

Multi-cloud, fine-tuning, extensive OCR, production availability commitments and WhatsApp Business are outside the base package. Additional channels, data preparation and integration work are separately scoped.

Your part

Provide a knowledge source you are authorised to use, accurate role permissions and a pilot owner. Approve data-processing terms, retention, suppliers and hosting region before deployment. Validate the pilot before expanding its use.

Illustrative output · not a customer result

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does private mean here?

Access and data handling are scoped for your organisation. The project identifies who processes content, which endpoints receive it, the storage region, supplier data-use terms and processing agreements. Private does not automatically mean EU-only or on-premises.

Is the complete AI chain end-to-end encrypted?

We specify TLS in transit and encryption at rest according to the architecture. We do not describe the complete path to an LLM as end-to-end encrypted without evidence that supports that claim.

Can we add WhatsApp or website chat?

Yes, subject to a separate scope, platform permissions, processing terms and supplier costs. The base pilot includes one agreed interface; it does not include WhatsApp Business.

Will it always answer correctly?

No. Source citation, permission checks, unsupported-answer refusal and agreed evaluation tests reduce risk. Important decisions remain subject to the human review defined for the use case.
