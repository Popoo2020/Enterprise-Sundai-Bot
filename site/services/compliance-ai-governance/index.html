FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which frameworks can we discuss?

ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 readiness, GDPR consulting, NIS2 readiness, DORA readiness and SOC 2 readiness, where applicable. The entry package covers one agreed framework; it does not assert that all of them apply to you.

Does this certify us?

No. ISO certification is issued by an appropriate certification body. SOC 2 is a separate professional examination and report. Readiness advice or a single penetration test cannot establish complete regulatory compliance.

Can you help with a DPIA?

A separately scoped DPIA covers one defined processing activity: processing description, necessity and proportionality, risks to people’s rights, mitigations, residual risk and follow-up ownership. It does not guarantee avoidance of fines.

Are CISO and DPO services included?

No. Fractional CISO is a security leadership service; DPO is a distinct privacy role. Each requires a suitable professional and a defined mandate. Independence and conflicts of interest must be checked before combining roles.
