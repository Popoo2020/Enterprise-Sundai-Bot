Q&A

Good questions. Straight answers.

Which frameworks can we discuss?

ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 readiness, GDPR consulting, NIS2 readiness, DORA readiness and SOC 2 readiness, where applicable. The entry package covers one agreed framework; it does not assert that all of them apply to you.

Does this certify us?

No. ISO certification is issued by an appropriate certification body. SOC 2 is a separate professional examination and report. Readiness advice or a single penetration test cannot establish complete regulatory compliance.

What does an ISO 27001 gap analysis or ISO 42001 readiness review cover?

The agreed sprint reviews one framework and up to ten control areas. ISO 27001 readiness focuses on the agreed information-security evidence; ISO 42001 readiness addresses the agreed AI-management evidence. Full implementation and certification are separate.

When is GDPR readiness consulting or DPIA support for AI processing useful?

GDPR consulting helps clarify the defined data-processing questions in scope. A separately scoped DPIA assessment examines one processing activity, risks to people and possible safeguards. Your organisation retains its decisions and ongoing responsibilities.

Can this help us with SOC 2 readiness consulting?

SOC 2 readiness can identify gaps in the agreed controls and evidence. A SOC 2 examination and assurance report are separate professional work; this service does not issue them.

Do we get security policy development or an AI policy?

Up to three draft documents can be included in the agreed sprint. Security policy development, an AI acceptable use policy or responsibility records should reflect your actual process and require your approval.

Is fractional CISO consulting or DPO support included?

Fractional CISO consulting is a separately defined leadership mandate. DPO support is a distinct privacy service requiring an appropriate professional and an independence review. Neither role is automatically included in the readiness sprint.

What should we prepare for a compliance readiness assessment?

Bring the product or process, relevant countries and sector, existing policies, supplier details and a decision-maker. We agree the framework, deliverables and acceptance criteria before work starts.
