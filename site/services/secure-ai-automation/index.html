FAQ

Clear expectations before a project begins.

Is this general software development?

No. The service focuses on bounded AI assistants, RAG systems, automations and decision-support workflows with a defined business purpose.

How is efficiency measured?

A baseline is agreed before the pilot. Measures can include time saved, shorter cycle time, reduced manual handling, consistency, quality and responsible adoption.

Can the work start with a small pilot?

Yes. A controlled pilot is often the preferred route before wider rollout, especially when data, security or organisational change need validation.
