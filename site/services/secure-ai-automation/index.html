Scope, responsibilities & boundaries

The fixed package

Secure AI Automation Sprint: one workflow, up to two systems with existing documented APIs, one trigger, one deployment environment and two refinement rounds. Acceptance criteria and operating costs are agreed before implementation.

Outside this scope

Creating an unavailable API, major system migration and unrestricted payments or consequential actions are excluded. Cloud, model, messaging and external licence costs are agreed separately with a budget or cap.

Your part

Provide authorised API access, a process owner and representative test data. Approve sensitive actions and the acceptance tests; retain responsibility for operational decisions and permissions.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does every workflow need AI?

No. Deterministic rules or API automation can be a better fit. We use an LLM only where it adds useful capability and can be bounded and tested.

Can it act without supervision?

Only for explicitly agreed low-risk actions. Payments and other material actions need the approval controls defined in the scope; a model-generated instruction alone is not authority to act.

What happens when a service fails?

The agreed design includes controlled retries, duplicate prevention, alerts and a fallback or human handoff. Recovery behaviour is tested against the acceptance criteria, rather than relying on silent success.
