FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which cloud identity controls are relevant?

Azure: Azure RBAC, Microsoft Entra ID, Conditional Access and Entra PIM. AWS: IAM policies and roles, IAM Identity Center, STS and permission boundaries where needed. Google Cloud: Cloud IAM roles and policy bindings, IAM Conditions and Privileged Access Manager. Controls depend on the selected provider and licensing. PIM/PAM manage privileged access; they are not alternative names for RBAC.

What does monthly Cloud Security Care include?

A separately agreed monthly service includes scheduled permission/configuration checks, alert review, small changes and a report, for up to six working hours per month against an agreed backlog. Additional hours require an approved service extension; they are not billed automatically.

Can you respond to an incident?

Initial response, evidence collection and specialist handoff may be agreed subject to availability. Emergency coverage and response commitments require appropriate staffing, an on-call agreement and an SLA; they are not included by default.
