Current 2026 guidance

The AI Act became generally applicable on 2 August 2026. Article 50 transparency obligations apply from that date. The major high-risk rules now follow later dates: 2 December 2027 for Annex III systems and 2 August 2028 for relevant product-integrated high-risk systems under Annex I. Readiness work should reflect this staged timeline rather than treating the Act as either fully delayed or fully identical for every AI use case.
