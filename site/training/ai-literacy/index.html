AI LITERACY TRAINING

AI Literacy Training for European Workplaces

Role-based AI literacy training for employees, managers and technical teams covering safe use, limitations, privacy, security and human judgement.

Direct answer

SundAI delivers role-based AI literacy training that helps employees, managers and technical teams understand appropriate use, limitations, data handling, security, verification, accountability and escalation.

Who this is for

Organisations that need practical, documented learning adapted to roles, tools and risk—not a generic introduction to AI.

What you receive

  • Role-based workshop or learning series
  • Relevant scenarios and exercises
  • Safe-use guidance and escalation paths
  • Participation and learning evidence

How the work runs

Identify roles, tools and learning needs.

Tailor scenarios and outcomes to the organisation.

Deliver interactive learning and practical exercises.

Document actions and reinforce good practice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this aligned with Article 4?

The training is designed to support role-based AI literacy measures under the EU AI Act, without claiming legal certification.

Can the engagement be adapted?

Yes. Scope, language, depth and delivery format are adjusted to the organisation, roles and risk context.

Does SundAI claim certification?

No. Work is standards-informed, but SundAI does not claim organisational ISO certification unless a valid certificate explicitly covers the organisation and scope.

These primary sources inform the work. They do not imply certification or endorsement.
