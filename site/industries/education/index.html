AI IN EDUCATION

Responsible AI for Education and Learning Organisations

AI governance, literacy and security for schools, colleges, training providers and learning organisations handling student, staff and assessment data.

Direct answer

SundAI helps education and learning organisations establish responsible AI use across staff, students, assessment, learning support, privacy, security, accessibility and AI literacy.

Who this is for

Schools, colleges, vocational providers, training organisations and education teams adopting generative AI or AI-enabled learning tools.

What you receive

  • Education AI policy and scenarios
  • Staff and leadership AI literacy
  • Student-data and supplier review
  • Assessment, accessibility and human oversight

How the work runs

Understand the sector, people and service context.

Assess data, rights, security and operational risk.

Design proportionate governance and practical controls.

Pilot carefully, measure outcomes and improve.

Frequently asked questions

Should students be prohibited from using AI?

Blanket bans often miss context. Clear learning goals, disclosure, assessment design and role-based guidance are more useful.

Can the engagement be adapted?

Yes. Scope, language, depth and delivery format are adjusted to the organisation, roles and risk context.

Does SundAI claim certification?

No. Work is standards-informed, but SundAI does not claim organisational ISO certification unless a valid certificate explicitly covers the organisation and scope.
