AI FOR EUROPEAN SMEs

Practical AI Governance and Security for European SMEs

Proportionate AI governance, security, supplier review, automation and literacy for European small and mid-sized organisations.

Direct answer

SundAI helps SMEs prioritise valuable AI use, manage supplier and data risk, set lightweight governance, train teams and move selected workflows into controlled operation.

Who this is for

European SMEs and growing organisations that need practical AI value but do not have a large compliance, security or AI governance function.

What you receive

  • Lean AI inventory and priority map
  • Named owners and lightweight policy baseline
  • Supplier and security review
  • Role-based literacy and implementation roadmap

How the work runs

Understand the sector, people and service context.

Assess data, rights, security and operational risk.

Design proportionate governance and practical controls.

Pilot carefully, measure outcomes and improve.

Frequently asked questions

Will governance create too much overhead?

It should not. Controls are prioritised according to actual use, risk and organisational capacity.

Can the engagement be adapted?

Yes. Scope, language, depth and delivery format are adjusted to the organisation, roles and risk context.

Does SundAI claim certification?

No. Work is standards-informed, but SundAI does not claim organisational ISO certification unless a valid certificate explicitly covers the organisation and scope.
