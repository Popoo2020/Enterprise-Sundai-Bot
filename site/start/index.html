COMMON QUESTIONS

Keep expectations precise.

Does a published service mean SundAI is immediately available?

No. Published service formats describe possible commercial scopes. Availability, contributors and timing are confirmed separately for each enquiry.

Does a named founder or contributor automatically perform the work?

No. A role shown on the website identifies organisational or authorship context. It does not by itself assign personal delivery, hours or availability for a particular engagement.

Does a Readiness Review certify compliance?

No. It can create a practical evidence baseline, priority gaps and an action plan. Legal interpretation, certification and system-specific assurance remain separate where required.
