COMMON QUESTIONS

Keep the buying decision simple.

Why start with a paid call?

It creates a bounded professional decision point rather than an open-ended sales meeting. If you continue to the Readiness Sprint within 30 days, the €490 fee is credited toward that Sprint.

Can we start directly with the Sprint?

Yes. If the scope is already clear, select the Readiness Sprint and describe the three use cases or tools you want reviewed.

Does the Sprint certify compliance?

No. It creates a practical evidence baseline, priority gaps and action plan. Legal interpretation, certification and system-specific assurance remain separate where required.
