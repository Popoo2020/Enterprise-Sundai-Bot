SHADOW AI ASSESSMENT

Shadow AI Assessment and Governance

Identify unofficial AI use, data exposure and policy gaps without blocking useful innovation. Get a proportionate risk register and action plan.

Direct answer

SundAI helps organisations identify unofficial AI tools and workflows, understand data and access exposure, distinguish useful practice from unacceptable risk, and create proportionate guidance and controls.

Who this is for

Organisations where employees already use public AI tools, browser extensions, copilots or unapproved assistants.

What you receive

  • Privacy-respecting discovery approach
  • Tool and use-case register
  • Data, access and supplier review
  • Approved-use pathways and action plan

How the work runs

Clarify the problem, scope and affected people.

Collect relevant technical and operational evidence.

Assess value, risk, controls and decision points.

Prioritise action and establish ownership.

Frequently asked questions

Is this employee surveillance?

No. The approach should be transparent, proportionate and focused on organisational risk rather than intrusive monitoring.

Can the engagement be adapted?

Yes. Scope, language, depth and delivery format are adjusted to the organisation, roles and risk context.

Does SundAI claim certification?

No. Work is standards-informed, but SundAI does not claim organisational ISO certification unless a valid certificate explicitly covers the organisation and scope.
