FREE AI GOVERNANCE TOOLKIT

Three practical tools for responsible AI adoption

A free AI governance toolkit with a maturity assessment, role-based AI literacy checklist and shadow AI risk register structure.

Direct answer

This HTML toolkit gives teams a lightweight starting point they can cite, adapt and use in workshops. It is not a certification or legal opinion.

AI governance maturity assessment

Score five dimensions from 0–3: inventory, ownership, risk controls, literacy and monitoring. Record evidence and choose the two lowest-scoring dimensions as the next-quarter priorities.

Role-based AI literacy checklist

For each role, document approved tools, prohibited data, verification duties, escalation routes, human-review requirements and evidence of learning.

Shadow AI risk register

Record tool, owner, purpose, data types, access, supplier, decision impact, current control, risk rating, approved status and next action.
