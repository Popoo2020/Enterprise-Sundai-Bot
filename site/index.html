Q&A

Good questions. Straight answers.

Where should we start?

Bring one repetitive task, one knowledge bottleneck or one security question. The free 20-minute call helps us identify the right starting point.

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

We start with the systems you already use. Integration options, access and any additional tools are checked before we agree the work.

How is the project priced?

We agree deliverables, acceptance criteria, timing and a fixed price before starting. Third-party costs and any later scope changes are agreed separately.

Who owns the system after handover?

Access, documentation, intellectual property, third-party rights and ongoing support are defined in the agreement. We make the handover explicit before work starts.

What happens after I request a call?

We reply to confirm availability and agree a time. The form sends a meeting request; it does not reserve a calendar slot.
