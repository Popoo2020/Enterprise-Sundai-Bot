Retrieved on 2026-10-13 from Google Fonts' official source repository.

| File | Family | Weights | Official source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sora-latin.woff2` | Sora | Variable 100–800 | https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sora | `sora-OFL.txt` |
| `manrope-latin.woff2` | Manrope | Variable 200–800 | https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope | `manrope-OFL.txt` |

Both webfonts were converted from the official variable TTF sources with fontTools. The subset includes Basic Latin, Latin-1 Supplement, Latin Extended-A and B, punctuation, the euro sign, trademark sign and directional arrows. It includes Danish and Swedish characters such as æ, ø, å, ä and ö. Full upstream SIL Open Font License texts are included next to the fonts.

Sora is a visual companion to SundAI's geometric wordmark. The original logo artwork remains unchanged. The logo's exact font has not been established; this choice is a visual interpretation, not a claim to have recovered its typeface. Manrope supports compact, readable body text and labels. Both fonts are self-hosted so pages do not need a remote font service.
